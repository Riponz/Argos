## Argos: No-nonsense finance

### Core Features
- Expense Entry
 - manually enter a transaction
- View All Entry
- Filter Payments
 - filter by:
  - month
  - time range
  - category
  - Payment Method

### Investment Features
- Investment Entry
 - Manually enter the details of the stock or sip

- View All Investments
- Investment Insights
 - Show total investment

### Dashboard & Insight
- Pie Chart
- Category Breakup
- Frequent Time
- Investment Summary

### Notification and Alerts
- Sip reminder
- Investement reminder

### Miscellaneous
- Recurring Expenses
 - automatically add to expenses
- Savings Tracker
 - AI for savings and expense analyser
